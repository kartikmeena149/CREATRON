# Editorial reels

This skill is based on frame-by-frame study of an editorial finance reel that Kartik calls a masterpiece. The
research is in Creatro's `docs/research/professional-storyboards.md`, section 2.1c. It is the second house
style, next to `explainer-reels`. Use this one when nobody is on camera and the story is about money, companies,
history or power. Build to these patterns with this video's own words and subject. Never copy another
creator's brand.

## The shape (50-70 s)

The reel alternates between two modes, like chapters:

1. **Hook, 0-3 s: a provocative collage.** The subject cut out (a person, a building, a product) over a
   colour wall, with a giant headline sitting *behind* them ("MOST POWERFUL COMPANY"). A slow push-in.
2. **Data mode: the claim, proved with type and simple charts** on warm paper. One fact per shot, 2-4 s each.
   Repeat the same layout with new names (a pattern of facts feels undeniable).
3. **The turn**, said quietly: a small italic line in the accent colour ("It all started with a devastating loss.").
4. **Story mode: cinematic scenes**, full bleed (a period re-creation, archive, a close-up), with small calm
   captions. Push in on the emotional beat and hold it a little too long.
5. **Year cards** as chapter markers: a tiny italic "So in" over a huge condensed "1988".
6. **Payoff: back to data mode** to bring it home to the viewer ("every time you buy an index fund…").
7. **Punchline on a picture**: the last words as big accent-coloured type over the final scene, then cut.

## Data mode: the look
- **Background**: warm light-grey paper with a soft radial vignette (about #E8E6E2 at the centre, #BDBAB5
  at the corners) and fine grain. No pure white, no gradients in colour.
- **One accent**: brick red (about #D2462F). Body type in a warm dark taupe (about #5B4A3B). Photos are
  black and white or muted, with the accent as a flat block (a red square over a face, multiplied).
- **Type is the picture.** The spoken words are set as the layout, so these shots need **no captions**:
  - Key words: **huge condensed sans capitals** (DIN Condensed Bold, Avenir Next Condensed Heavy, Futura
    Condensed ExtraBold), in red or taupe, tight tracking.
  - Connecting words: **small italic serif** (Bodoni 72 Book Italic, Didot Italic, Baskerville Italic), in
    sentence case, 4-10× smaller than the key word, tucked against it ("in assets" under "$10 TRILLION.",
    "They are the" over "TOP").
  - Words appear **one at a time on the voice**. A long line first appears with wide spaces between words,
    then closes up as the sentence ends.
- **Numbers are objects**: $10 trillion becomes pallets of cash, a share becomes a ring. Give every huge
  number a physical form, or a comparison with a famous person or company.
- **Ring charts for "who owns more"**: concentric arcs around a logo, one per owner, drawing on in their
  colours (accent = the subject, taupe = the other), with a small legend (a colour square plus a label, or a
  tiny photo plus a label). Reuse the same chart for the next company.
- **Photo cards**: a photo as a clean rectangle on the paper (no frame, a soft shadow), with a small serif line
  above it.
- **Collage for an event**: 4-5 archive photo cards (newspaper, crowd, trading floor) floating around one red
  line ("2008 CRISIS HIT"), drifting slowly.

## Story mode: the look
- Full-bleed cinematic scenes: warm, contrasty, period-correct, shallow depth of field. Recreations,
  archive or B-roll, never stock that looks like stock.
- **Captions**: one small line, white sentence case, centred mid-frame, with a soft shadow. Calmer than the
  type in data mode.
- Camera: slow push-ins, a punch-in on the worst moment, a long hold on the face.

## Sound
- A slow, minimal bed (piano, low strings) that **drops to a single note at the turn**.
- A soft tick for each word that appears in data mode, a sub-bass hit on year cards and hero numbers.
- Paper and whoosh sounds when cards land. Room tone and murmur in story scenes (phones, trading floor).
- Silence before the punchline.

## Pacing
- Data mode: one fact per 2-4 s, at a steady rhythm. Story mode: slower, 3-4 s, hold the emotion.
- Hard cuts between modes. No transitions in the edit; the type itself moves.

## Done means
- [ ] Hook: a collage with the headline behind the subject, pushing in, in the first 3 s.
- [ ] Data mode on warm paper: condensed capitals plus small italic serif, one accent, no captions over type shots.
- [ ] Every big number is an object or a ring chart, and a repeated layout makes the pattern felt.
- [ ] A quiet turn, then story mode in cinematic scenes with small captions.
- [ ] Year cards mark the jumps in time.
- [ ] Back to data mode for the viewer's own stake; the punchline is type over the final picture.
- [ ] Sound: word ticks, sub hits on years, the bed drops at the turn, silence before the end.
