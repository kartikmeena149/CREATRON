Creatro Film with thisAtharv

( Rules )

Three rules it is built on.

( 01 )

The model does the editing.

The hook, the cuts, the pacing and the style are decided by the AI model you pick: Claude, or any model through OpenRouter, free ones included.

( 02 )

A broken tool never ends the job.

Every tool Creatro uses has a backup. If one fails, it says so in one line, switches to the backup and finishes your video.

( 03 )

You get a project, not a file.

Every AI edit is an open folder on your Mac. Your source is never changed, cuts and captions stay editable, and one command exports it again.

( Editor )

Everything a video needs, nothing it doesn't.

Viewer

Live from the original.

Cuts, speed, zoom, the 9:16 crop and captions, straight from your file. Nothing to render while you work.

Timeline

Cut by hand, any time.

Drag clip edges, split with S, undo anything. Rough Cut keeps the spoken lines and drops dead air and retakes.

Captions

Captions stay text.

Timed to the speech, so they survive every re-cut. Edit a word or restyle them until the final export.

Voice

Your voice, on your Mac.

Clone a voice from a clip with consent, write a script, and generate the voiceover locally.

Auto edit

Claude edits it all.

Give it your footage and a brief. It makes the whole video and hands back the project, ready to change.

Import

Open Claude's videos.

Bring in a HyperFrames or Remotion video and every layer and keyframe becomes editable on the timeline.
